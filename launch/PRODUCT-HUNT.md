First comment:
Hi Product Hunt! I built Cath Lab with GPT-6-Astra to give students and early-career trainees a hands-on introduction to coronary catheter movement.

You can explore 3D and simulated fluoroscopy, use camera hand controls, and review movement graphs with CSV/JSON exports. The simulator runs in your browser without an account, in English and Japanese. The source code is also available for local use.

The core simulator is free. A hosted Jev-assisted hand-intent classification plan is planned at $5/month through Stripe. Subscriptions are not open yet. Jev receives movement summaries, not camera frames. We have not established improvements in precision or latency from Jev.

This is a learning prototype, not a clinically validated simulator or a tool for clinical decisions. The research vascular dataset has separate use restrictions, which we are resolving before paid hosting.

I would especially value feedback on whether the relationship between hand movement and catheter movement is understandable.
